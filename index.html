Privacy Policy

App: Halal Finder: Food Scanner  |  Last updated: April 2026

Skyloop Apps ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Halal Finder: Food Scanner mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

a) Camera

The App requests camera permission solely to scan product barcodes. No photos, images, or video are stored on our servers or transmitted anywhere.

b) Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show ads in the free version of the App. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device make, model, and OS version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • App usage signals for ad relevance

This data is processed by Google under their own Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

c) Local App Data

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Scan history (barcodes you have scanned)
  • Scan counter (number of scans)
  • Theme preference (light / dark)
  • Ad consent choice (GDPR / CCPA)

d) Product Lookups

When you scan a barcode, the App sends the barcode number to our product database API to retrieve halal classification information. No personal identifiers are attached to these requests.

e) In-App Purchases (iOS only)

On iOS, optional premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment card details. Purchase validation is handled via RevenueCat under their Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display halal / haram classification results for scanned products
  • To deliver advertising that supports the free version of the App
  • To remember your scan history and preferences locally on your device
  • To validate in-app purchases (iOS only)

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal data to third parties.

3. Advertising and Consent

EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we will present a consent dialog (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before initialising any ads. You may choose to receive personalised ads or non-personalised ads, or withdraw consent at any time through Settings → Manage Ad Consent in the App.

United States (CCPA)

California residents have the right to opt out of the "sale" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. The advertising identifier collected by AdMob is used solely for ad delivery. To opt out of personalised advertising, go to your device settings and reset or limit the advertising identifier.

All Other Users

Outside of the EEA/UK/Switzerland/USA, personalised ads are shown by default using the advertising identifier. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings at any time.

4. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

The App's content rating is suitable for all ages. The tagForUnderAgeOfConsent flag is set to false in the advertising SDK, and the App is not designated as child-directed.

5. Data Retention

Scan history is stored on your device indefinitely until you clear it within the App or uninstall the App. We do not retain personal data on our servers.

6. Data Security

All network requests use HTTPS. Barcode scanning and halal classification are performed without linking results to any personal identifier.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold. Since we do not store personal data on our servers, you can exercise most rights by clearing your scan history in the App or uninstalling it. For advertising-related data, please refer to Google's and RevenueCat's respective privacy controls.

To withdraw GDPR/CCPA consent, go to Settings → Manage Ad Consent in the App, or visit adssettings.google.com.
